# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** [EFFECTIVE_DATE]

CraveCircle ("CraveCircle", "we", "us") is a cooking app that turns cooking videos into structured recipes and lets you save, organize, and share them. This Privacy Policy explains what information the CraveCircle iOS app handles, how it is used, and the choices you have. It applies to the CraveCircle mobile app operated by [LEGAL_ENTITY].

**The short version:** CraveCircle is built to keep your data on your device. Recipe transcription, on-screen-text reading, hand-gesture detection, and voice commands all run **on your iPhone**. We do not use third-party analytics or advertising trackers, and we never record or upload your camera or microphone.

## 1. Information we handle

**Account information.** If you create an account, we store your email address and basic profile details (such as a display name) to sign you in and associate your content with you.

**Content you create.** Recipes you import or write, photos you add to a recipe, collections, grocery lists, likes, saves, and comments. This is stored on your device and, when you are signed in, in your CraveCircle account so it is available to you across sessions.

**Camera, microphone, speech, and photo inputs.** With your permission, the app uses:
- **Camera** — to detect a hand-swipe in hands-free "Flashcard" cooking mode. Camera frames are analyzed in real time on your device and are **never recorded, stored, or transmitted**. No camera preview is displayed.
- **Microphone** — to recognize the spoken commands "next" and "back" in Flashcard mode. Audio is processed on your device for those keywords and is **not recorded or uploaded**.
- **Speech recognition** — to transcribe a cooking video's spoken audio into recipe steps, using Apple's on-device speech frameworks.
- **Photo library** — to let you pick a cooking video to import, or a photo to attach to a recipe you create.

We do **not** collect precise location, contacts, health data, or advertising identifiers, and we do **not** track you across other apps or websites.

## 2. How we use information
- To provide the app: sign you in, show and sync your recipes and lists, and run recipe imports.
- To operate the two import methods described in Section 4.
- To respond to your support requests.

We do **not** sell your personal information, and we do **not** share it with advertisers.

## 3. On-device processing (how CraveCircle protects your privacy)
The features that use the camera, microphone, speech, and video all run **locally on your iPhone**:
- **On-device recipe import** ("Pick a cooking video"): the video's audio is transcribed and any on-screen text is read (using Apple's on-device Speech and Vision technology) **entirely on your device**. The video is processed and then **discarded** — it is not stored or uploaded.
- **Hands-free navigation**: hand-gesture and voice detection happen in real time on the device; nothing is recorded.

## 4. When information leaves your device
Information leaves your device only in these cases:
- **Link / Share import.** When you paste a link or share a video from another app (for example Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, or Facebook), the **link (URL)** is sent to the CraveCircle backend so it can fetch that public video, transcribe it, and return the recipe text. The fetched video is transcribed and then **discarded — it is not stored**. This backend is operated by CraveCircle (self-hosted), not a third-party cloud analytics service.
- **Account and content sync.** When you are signed in, your account and the content you create are stored in your CraveCircle account so they persist and sync.

## 5. Third-party services
- **Apple** provides the on-device Speech, Vision, and camera frameworks. This processing happens on your device; the results are not sent to us as media.
- **Supabase** provides authentication and database storage for accounts and content when you sign in.
- **Source video platforms.** When you choose to import a link, the app fetches the specific public post you selected. Please respect the terms of the platform you import from.

We do not integrate advertising SDKs or third-party analytics/tracking SDKs.

## 6. Data retention and deletion
Your content is kept while your account is active. You can delete individual recipes, collections, grocery items, and comments in the app. To delete your account and associated content, contact us at [SUPPORT_EMAIL] and we will process the request.

## 7. Children
CraveCircle is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 8. Your rights
Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information. To exercise these rights, contact [SUPPORT_EMAIL]. We will respond within a reasonable time and as required by applicable law.

## 9. Security
We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but on-device processing means most sensitive inputs (camera, microphone, video) never leave your phone.

## 10. Changes to this policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date above and, where appropriate, notified in the app.

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I turn a cooking video into a recipe?**
Open Import and either **pick a cooking video** from your phone or **paste/share a link** from Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, or Facebook. CraveCircle reads the spoken steps and any on-screen text and builds a structured recipe.

**What's the difference between "pick a video" and pasting a link?**
Picking a video runs **entirely on your iPhone** — nothing is uploaded, and the video is discarded after the recipe is made. Pasting/sharing a link sends just the **URL** to the CraveCircle service to fetch and transcribe that public video (also discarded after processing).

**Why does the app ask for camera and microphone access?**
Only for **hands-free Flashcard cooking**: the camera detects a hand-swipe to change steps, and the microphone lets you say "next" or "back". These run on your device and are **never recorded or uploaded**. You can decline them and still use the app with touch.

**Why does import sometimes say a video needs a sign-in?**
Some links (for example private or age-restricted posts) can't be downloaded without being logged in on the source platform. Try a public post, or use on-device import.

**How do I delete my data or account?**
Delete individual items in the app, or email **[SUPPORT_EMAIL]** to delete your account and associated content.
